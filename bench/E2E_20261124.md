# End-to-end round trips, 24 Nov 2026 (release check)

Box: 8 vCPU / 15 GB Linux VM, shared with other jobs while these ran (CLI and web runs partly overlapped), so the
times are only a smoke check, not benchmarks. Inputs: the first 20 MB of enwik8, eight 8 MB slices of public CSVs
(Data8277, Panel_2000_2022, TRI_By_Name, fndds, hctsa, products, records, youth) and the full records100k.csv.
"OK" = the restored file's sha256 equals the original's. Sizes are the downloaded `.qc.gz` container (JSON + base64 +
gzip around the codec output, about +1% over the raw codec output).

## v1.0.0 re-check: `.qz` container (box, 24 Nov 2026, 9:40–10:10 AM CST)

The `.qc.gz` sizes above include about 1% of container overhead (JSON with a base64 payload, then gzip: gzip
cannot fully undo base64 on already-compressed data). v1.0.0 writes `.qz`: a ~780-byte JSON header followed by the
raw codec output. Same encode, both containers:
